cat ./about/aphantasia.txt

Aphantasia

One of the things that has had a major effect on the way I approach creative work is something called aphantasia.

Aphantasia in short is "The inability to consciously imagine" meaning, in my sleep I can dream, but whilst awake I have the inability to visualise something as simple as an apple. In the past I've gotten lots of negative remarks, i.e "How can you be creative with the inability to imagine" which is exactly the point I am fighting, and it's been working out pretty well.
