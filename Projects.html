nagukab/ [3D • Adventure • Game Jam]

Nagukab

A short 3D adventure game built around the theme “Less Is More.” You play as a robot with limited energy, forcing you to carefully manage your resources while dealing with enemies, harvesting energy from crates and repairing doors.

All of the game's assets and animations were made by me. Coding was assisted by Qwen 3.7, while the sound effects were sourced from Pixabay under free-use licensing.
